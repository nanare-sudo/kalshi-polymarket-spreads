How pairs are verified

Matching prediction markets by string similarity produces confident nonsense — “Trump Jr.” vs. “Trump”, “nominee” vs. “election winner”, “cuts by 25bp” vs. “cuts”. The pipeline runs three stages: blocking by category and close-date window, IDF-weighted token overlap computed over the loaded universe, then rule verification of numeric thresholds, dates and entity contrasts. Each pair carries a confidence score (median 0.83 in this scan) and notes on what still differs. Full methodology →
